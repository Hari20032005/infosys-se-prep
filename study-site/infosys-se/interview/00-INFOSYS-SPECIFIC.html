3. What Infosys says about its direction

Infosys describes Topaz as “an AI-first set of services, solutions and platforms” on its own site (https://www.infosys.com/services/data-ai-topaz.html, read through a search result on 2026-09-30). A 2026 Specialist Programmer job description lists “AI & Agentic Engineering” and “Apply AI in software engineering” (Infosys SP JD, in the 2026-cycle placement documents). Neither is a description of the Systems Engineer test; both explain the drive email's “AI-driven vision”.

4. Questions worth asking at the end of the interview

  1. “What does the first year look like after training: how are projects allocated?”
  2. “How do trainees use AI tools in day-to-day delivery?”
  3. “What skills would you advise me to build during the training weeks?”

Do not ask about salary, leave or work-from-home in the interview.

5. Why Infosys? Building blocks

Pick two that are true for you and that you can back with one sentence each: structured residential training in Mysuru; work on varied clients and technologies (which the official eligibility text itself mentions); the AI-first direction; a clear upgrade route to higher roles for people who want it. Avoid “large company”, “brand” and “job security”.
