Infosys SE/Core Course: start here

Core Course: start here

Twenty-two short lessons on the topics that carry about 58 of the 75 marks. Reading and practice take about 19 hours in all.

What this is

Every lesson explains one topic in plain words, shows the method, works five examples through, and then gives you 15 practice questions in three levels. Level 3 is harder than anything in the 2020 official sample, on purpose. Every answer to a number, logic or code question was checked by running code; the English questions are marked as judgement and tied to a page I opened and read.

The lessons are in a good order for most people, but you do not have to go straight through. Use the diagnostic below to find where to start.

The test at a glance

The planning model is 7 timed sections, 60 questions, 120 minutes, 75 marks plus an essay: reasoning 15, technical (maths) 10, verbal 20, pseudocode 5, numerical puzzle 4, grammar 5, writing 1 [LIKELY]. Every section has its own cut-off, the value is not known, there is no coding and no calculator [CONFIRMED]. The full evidence is in the pattern synthesis. Nobody has yet reported the 2027-batch test itself, so the section counts are the best current estimate and not a promise.

Candidates who posted on Reddit about the October 2025 drives call the written test easy, and two of them say puzzles and pseudocode are where people miss a section cut-off [REPORTED]. That fits the model above and changes no lesson. The method and the limits are in the Reddit research.

How to use a lesson

  1. Read the idea and what Infosys actually asks (five minutes).
  2. Read the method and the shortcuts, then follow the five worked examples with a pencil.
  3. Do Level 1. If you miss more than one, re-read the method.
  4. Do Level 2 against the clock. This is the level of the real test.
  5. Do Level 3 when Level 2 feels easy.
  6. Last, read the 60-second recap. Come back to it the night before.

A 30-minute diagnostic

Do these 12 questions in 30 minutes, with no help. They are the first Level 2 question of each lesson named. Check the answers in each lesson's key.

Technical (maths) Data interpretation, Level 2, question 6 · Linear equations, ages and heads-and-legs, Level 2, question 6

Score each section out of 2. If you got 0, do that section's lessons first, in order. If you got 1, do them after the first group. If you got 2, read the recaps and go straight to Level 3.

After the test

Selection is not the end. Candidates report a long and uneven wait for the offer letter, and a pre-test in the first week at Mysuru that covers aptitude, English, Java, DBMS and OOP. When you have time, read what happens after you are selected. The optional DSE and SP upgrade is a different, coding-heavy test; what candidates report about it is there so you can decide whether to try it.

When time is short

  • Three days left: do the Tier 1 lessons (the first column of the weightage analysis) and read the recap of every other lesson.
  • A week left: do all the lessons once, Level 2 only, and then go to the last 48 hours page.
  • Two days left: go straight to the last 48 hours.
