A21 — Non-verbal reasoning

Tier 2 · Reasoning Ability · 1.4% of marks · about 0.4 h · confidence Medium

1. Why this topic matters here

Expect about 1.1 questions and 1.1 marks on this topic (about 1.4% of the 75 scored marks). It sits in Reasoning Ability, part of the 25-question aptitude block (Reasoning Ability 15 Q / 25 min + Technical Ability 10 Q / 35 min, one cut-off each). Tier 2, 0.4 hours to reach reliable recall, confidence Medium.

No Infosys question on this topic was recovered. It rests on syllabus lists or first-hand naming only (B7, C1), so its share is an allocation, not an observation.

Why the tier: expected marks per study hour, weighted by confidence, is 2.10 against a paper average of 1.84 (../data/analysis-tables.md §6). Every section has its own cut-off, so a zero here costs the whole section's chance, not only these marks. There is no calculator. Negative marking: none reported.

2. Exactly what gets asked

Tier 4, recognise-only. “Non-verbal reasoning” is on the 2025 deck's list (C1) [REPORTED]. On a text-only page we can drill only the parts that do not need a figure: counting cubes and squares, dice, clocks. Figure series, paper folding and embedded figures need pictures; practise those from the linked sites.

Sub-pattern How it appears
Painted cube cut into small cubes How many have exactly one face painted?
Dice Opposite faces add to 7
Mirror and water images Mirror image of a clock time
Counting figures Squares or rectangles in a grid

3. Concept notes

  • Cube n×n×n cut into unit cubes: 3 faces painted: 8; 2 faces: 12(n−2); 1 face: 6(n−2)²; no face: (n−2)³.
  • Dice: opposite faces sum to 7 on a standard die.
  • Mirror image of a clock time: subtract the time from 12:00 (or 11:60).
  • Squares in an n×n grid: 1² + 2² + … + n²; rectangles: [n(n+1)/2]².

4. Must-memorise block

n 3 faces 2 faces 1 face 0 faces
3 8 12 6 1
4 8 24 24 8
5 8 36 54 27

5. Worked examples

Example 1. A cube cut into 4³ = 64 pieces: exactly one face painted = 6 × (4−2)² = 24.

Example 2. The face with 2 is on top of a standard die: the bottom shows 7 − 2 = 5.

Example 3. Mirror image of 3:15: 12:00 − 3:15 = 8:45.

Example 4. Squares in a 4×4 grid: 16 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 30.

6. Traps and gotchas

Trap Why it catches people How to avoid it
Counting corners in “two faces” Corners have three Edges only, minus corners
Using 7 for unusual dice Some dice differ Standard die unless told otherwise
Mirror time as 12 − hour only Minutes matter Subtract the full time

7. Practice questions

More questions: QB-A21-non-verbal-reasoning.md.

1. [Dice] On a standard die opposite faces add up to 7. If the face showing 2 is on top, which number is on the bottom? a) 3 b) 5 c) 4 d) 1

2. [Counting figures] How many rectangles (squares included) can be traced along the lines of an 3 × 3 grid? a) 36 b) 27 c) 33 d) 14

3. [Dice] On a standard die opposite faces add up to 7. If the face showing 6 is on top, which number is on the bottom? a) 1 b) 4 c) 5 d) 6

4. [Painted cube] A solid cube is painted on all six faces and then cut into 3³ = 27 identical small cubes. How many small cubes have one painted face? a) 54 b) 36 c) 1 d) 6

5. [Dice] On a standard die opposite faces add up to 7. If the face showing 5 is on top, which number is on the bottom? a) 3 b) 4 c) 2 d) 6

6. [Mirror clock] A clock shows 2:50. What time will its mirror image show? a) 9:20 b) 9:10 c) 9:05 d) 9:15

7. [Mirror clock] A clock shows 1:40. What time will its mirror image show? a) 10:15 b) 10:30 c) 10:20 d) 10:25

8. [Painted cube] A solid cube is painted on all six faces and then cut into 4³ = 64 identical small cubes. How many small cubes have no painted faces? a) 96 b) 4 c) 48 d) 8

ANSWER KEY

  1. b — Opposite faces sum to 7, so the bottom is 7 − 2 = 5.
  2. a — Choose 2 of the 4 vertical lines and 2 of the 4 horizontal lines: C(4,2)² = 6² = 36.
  3. a — Opposite faces sum to 7, so the bottom is 7 − 6 = 1.
  4. d — Face-centre cubes: 6 faces × (3 − 2)² = 6.
  5. c — Opposite faces sum to 7, so the bottom is 7 − 5 = 2.
  6. b — The mirror image time is 12:00 − 2:50 = 9:10 (subtract from 12 hours; for 12-hour clocks this is the same as 11:60 − t). Answer 9:10.
  7. c — The mirror image time is 12:00 − 1:40 = 10:20 (subtract from 12 hours; for 12-hour clocks this is the same as 11:60 − t). Answer 10:20.
  8. d — Interior cubes: (4 − 2)³ = 8.
