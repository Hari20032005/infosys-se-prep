99 — Study Plan

The evaluation date is not stated. The drive email gives none, and coaches expect campus drives in late September or early October 2026 [REPORTED]. Your invitation (sent to your personal email after you complete the Application Form) will name the date; until then keep the 3-day plan ready. Hours below come from data/weightage.json, so every day's total is the sum of its rows.

Pick a plan by days left

Days left Plan Roughly
1–4 3-day plan Tier 1 plus a mock and interview basics
5–9 7-day plan Tier 1 and 2, most of Tier 3, one mock, the whole interview
10 or more 14-day plan Everything, two mocks, spare hours for revision

Rules for every plan. (1) Every section has its own cut-off, so never abandon a section to save another. (2) Do each bank timed at the real pace (03-TEST-STRATEGY.md §2). (3) After each mock, study the section you scored lowest in, not the one you like. (4) Sleep the night before; nothing in this library is worth a tired morning.

3-day plan

For when the test is within about four days. Tier 1 is 50 of the 75 expected marks (15 topics); this plan covers all of it, plus two short Tier 2 topics and the interview basics. It is heavy: about 8 hours a day. With fewer hours, cut in this order: the Day 3 interview rows, A3 and A5, the timed essay; never cut the mock.
