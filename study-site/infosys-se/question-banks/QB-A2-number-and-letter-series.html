  3. a — The terms are squares: 21², 12², 31², 13², 41², 14², 51². The roots alternate between a number and its digit reversal (21, 12, 31, 13, …). After 51² comes 15² = 225.
  4. b — The terms are squares of consecutive primes: 13, 17, 19, 23, 29 → the next prime is 31, giving 961.
  5. b — Two series are woven together. Odd places: 84, 68, 52, 36 (−16 each). Even places: 11, 19, 27 (+8 each). The next place is an even place: 27 + 8 = 35.
  6. d — The terms are consecutive primes: 19, 23, 29, 31, 37 → the next prime is 41, giving 41.
  7. c — The terms are squares: 16², 61², 17², 71², 18², 81², 19². The roots alternate between a number and its digit reversal (16, 61, 17, 71, …). After 19² comes 91² = 8281.
  8. b — Three tracks. Numbers: 15, 24, 35, 48, 63, next 80. Letters step back two places each time: R, P, N, L, J, next H. Last numbers rise by 4: 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, next 26. Answer 80H26.
  9. b — each term is the sum of the two before it: 13 and 21 give 34.
  10. d — The terms are squares: 12², 21², 13², 31², 14², 41², 15². The roots alternate between a number and its digit reversal (12, 21, 13, 31, …). After 15² comes 51² = 2601.
