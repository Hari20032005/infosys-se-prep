24. [Frequency table] The heights of 407 students are grouped as shown.

Height (cm) Students
140–150 77
150–160 39
160–170 79
170–180 106
180–190 106

What percentage of the students are shorter than 160 cm? a) 28.5% b) 71.5% c) 38.54% d) 9.58%

ANSWER KEY

  1. c — Students in the first 4 groups: 92 + 26 + 25 + 39 = 182. Percentage = 182/204 × 100 = 89.22%.
  2. a — Change = 890 − 800 = 90. As a percentage of the starting year: 90/800 × 100 = 11.25%.
  3. c — Convert each share to marks (400 × share): Maths 120, Geography 100, History 80, Science 60, Hindi 40. Only Hindi is below 60. Best − worst = 120 − 40 = 80.
  4. d — Convert each share to marks (600 × share): Geography 240, History 150, Maths 90, Hindi 72, English 48. Only English is below 50. Best − worst = 240 − 48 = 192.
  5. b — 430 : 600, divide both by 10 → 43:60.
  6. a — Total = 350 + 560 + 800 + 360 + 800 = 2870; average = 2870/5 = 574.
  7. d — Students in the first 2 groups: 76 + 35 = 111. Percentage = 111/335 × 100 = 33.13%.
  8. a — Students in the first 3 groups: 60 + 48 + 22 = 130. Percentage = 130/314 × 100 = 41.4%.
  9. c — Students in the first 4 groups: 92 + 38 + 48 + 111 = 289. Percentage = 289/319 × 100 = 90.6%.
  10. b — Students in the first 4 groups: 37 + 105 + 85 + 43 = 270. Percentage = 270/309 × 100 = 87.38%.
  11. d — Students in the first 3 groups: 40 + 28 + 115 = 183. Percentage = 183/277 × 100 = 66.06%.
  12. d — Students in the first 3 groups: 38 + 36 + 42 = 116. Percentage = 116/259 × 100 = 44.79%.
  13. a — Students in the first 2 groups: 56 + 69 = 125. Percentage = 125/385 × 100 = 32.47%.
  14. d — Students in the first 3 groups: 24 + 45 + 50 = 119. Percentage = 119/306 × 100 = 38.89%.
  15. a — Students in the first 2 groups: 88 + 33 = 121. Percentage = 121/376 × 100 = 32.18%.
  16. b — Students in the first 3 groups: 102 + 58 + 32 = 192. Percentage = 192/361 × 100 = 53.19%.
  17. b — Total = 450 + 460 + 220 + 260 + 730 = 2120; average = 2120/5 = 424.
  18. c — 760 : 450, divide both by 10 → 76:45.
  19. b — Students in the first 2 groups: 41 + 95 = 136. Percentage = 136/379 × 100 = 35.88%.
  20. b — Convert each share to marks (400 × share): Hindi 88, Maths 112, Science 80, English 72, Geography 48. Only Geography is below 50. Best − worst = 112 − 48 = 64.
  21. c — Students in the first 2 groups: 91 + 118 = 209. Percentage = 209/506 × 100 = 41.3%.
  22. d — Combined = 510 + 350 = 860. 510/860 × 100 = 59.3%.
  23. c — Students in the first 4 groups: 49 + 78 + 36 + 82 = 245. Percentage = 245/265 × 100 = 92.45%.
  24. a — Students in the first 2 groups: 77 + 39 = 116. Percentage = 116/407 × 100 = 28.5%.
