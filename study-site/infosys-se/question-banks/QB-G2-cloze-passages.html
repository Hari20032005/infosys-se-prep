QB-G2 — Cloze passages (five blanks each)

English Grammar · Tier 1 · 25 questions · topic file: see the G2 file in topics/, puzzles/, pseudocode/ or interview/

Five passages of five blanks each, the shape of the English Grammar section (5 questions, 10 minutes). Hand-written for this library.

How to use this bank. Attempt every question before opening the key. Answer letters are balanced across a–d and not in a repeating pattern, so guessing the pattern will not help.

Practice questions

Passage 1. Choose the word or phrase that best fills each numbered blank.

Artificial intelligence (1) ___ changing the way companies work. Many routine tasks (2) ___ now done by software, which leaves employees (3) ___ time for creative work. (4) ___ some people fear losing their jobs, experts say new roles will appear. Workers who keep learning (5) ___ best prepared for the change.

1. [Cloze] Blank (1) of passage 1: a) are b) were c) be d) is

2. [Cloze] Blank (2) of passage 1: a) was b) are c) has d) is

3. [Cloze] Blank (3) of passage 1: a) many b) lot c) most d) more

4. [Cloze] Blank (4) of passage 1: a) Unless b) Although c) Because d) Until

5. [Cloze] Blank (5) of passage 1: a) be b) been c) are d) is

Passage 2. Choose the word or phrase that best fills each numbered blank.

Water is (1) ___ of the most important resources on Earth. Yet millions of people still do not have access (2) ___ clean drinking water. In many cities, pipes (3) ___ old and leak a large amount every day. The government (4) ___ announced a plan to repair them by next year. If everyone (5) ___ water carefully, the problem can be reduced.

6. [Cloze] Blank (1) of passage 2: a) a b) an c) the d) one

7. [Cloze] Blank (2) of passage 2: a) at b) of c) to d) for

8. [Cloze] Blank (3) of passage 2: a) are b) is c) has been d) was

9. [Cloze] Blank (4) of passage 2: a) has b) is c) have d) had

10. [Cloze] Blank (5) of passage 2: a) will use b) use c) uses d) used

Passage 3. Choose the word or phrase that best fills each numbered blank.

Last month I travelled (1) ___ Mysuru to attend a training programme. The train was scheduled (2) ___ at 6 a.m., so I woke up very early. When I reached the station, the train (3) ___ already left. I had to wait (4) ___ three hours for the next one. This experience taught me (5) ___ always plan my journey in advance.

11. [Cloze] Blank (1) of passage 3: a) in b) at c) on d) to

12. [Cloze] Blank (2) of passage 3: a) to leave b) leaving c) leave d) left

13. [Cloze] Blank (3) of passage 3: a) have b) was c) has d) had

14. [Cloze] Blank (4) of passage 3: a) for b) since c) during d) from

15. [Cloze] Blank (5) of passage 3: a) that b) to c) for d) about

Passage 4. Choose the word or phrase that best fills each numbered blank.

Regular exercise is good (1) ___ both the body and the mind. Doctors recommend (2) ___ least thirty minutes of activity every day. People (3) ___ exercise regularly usually sleep better and feel less stressed. Unfortunately, many of (4) ___ spend long hours at a desk and move very little. A short walk after lunch (5) ___ make a big difference.

16. [Cloze] Blank (1) of passage 4: a) for b) of c) at d) to

17. [Cloze] Blank (2) of passage 4: a) on b) in c) by d) at

18. [Cloze] Blank (3) of passage 4: a) which b) who c) whom d) whose

19. [Cloze] Blank (4) of passage 4: a) we b) ourselves c) us d) our

20. [Cloze] Blank (5) of passage 4: a) has b) can c) is d) may be

Passage 5. Choose the word or phrase that best fills each numbered blank.

Online learning has become popular (1) ___ the last few years. Students can now attend classes from (2) ___ homes and watch lectures again whenever they wish. However, it (3) ___ self-discipline. A student who (4) ___ distracted easily may fall behind. (5) ___ , good teachers use short quizzes to keep learners engaged.

21. [Cloze] Blank (1) of passage 5: a) since b) by c) in d) at

22. [Cloze] Blank (2) of passage 5: a) their b) them c) they d) theirs

23. [Cloze] Blank (3) of passage 5: a) required b) requires c) require d) requiring

24. [Cloze] Blank (4) of passage 5: a) get b) getting c) gets d) have got

25. [Cloze] Blank (5) of passage 5: a) Therefore b) Because c) Although d) Unless

ANSWER KEY

  1. d — Artificial intelligence is singular, and the sentence describes something happening now: is changing.
  2. b — Tasks is plural and now fixes the present: are done.
  3. d — Leaves employees more time: the comparative more.
  4. b — The two clauses contrast (fear vs. new roles): Although.
  5. c — Workers … are best prepared: plural subject, present tense.
  6. d — The fixed phrase is one of the most …: one.
  7. c — The noun access takes to.
  8. a — Pipes is plural and the passage is in the present: are old.
  9. a — A singular subject and a plan announced for the future: present perfect has announced.
  10. c — In an if-clause the present simple is used, and everyone is singular: uses.
  11. d — You travel to a place.
  12. a — Was scheduled is followed by an infinitive: to leave.
  13. d — The train left before I arrived: two past events, the earlier one in the past perfect, had already left.
  14. a — A length of time after wait: for three hours.
  15. b — Taught me to always plan (infinitive of purpose): to.
  16. a — The fixed pair is good for.
  17. d — The fixed phrase is at least.
  18. b — People who exercise: a relative pronoun for people, subject of exercise: who.
  19. c — After the preposition of use the object form: many of us.
  20. b — A modal is followed by the base verb: can make.
  21. c — In the last few years. (Since needs a point in time.)
  22. a — A possessive before a noun: their homes.
  23. b — It is singular and the statement is general: requires.
  24. c — Singular subject student, present tense: gets.
  25. a — The teachers' response follows from the problem just described: Therefore.
