# 00 — Master Index: Infosys Systems Engineer (Trainee), campus 2026-27

A self-contained preparation library built from evidence, not from memory. **Start with `99-STUDY-PLAN.md`** and the days you have left.

## What the test is (planning model, confidence Medium)

Seven timed sections, **60 questions, 120 minutes, 75 marks plus an essay**, in person on the college lab computers, **no coding**, **no calculator**, a cut-off in every section. Details, the evidence and the conflicts: `research/03-pattern-synthesis.md`.

## How much to trust it

Tags used throughout: `[CONFIRMED]` three or more independent sources or an official document; `[LIKELY]` two; `[REPORTED]` one; `[INFERRED]`; `[UNVERIFIED — model inference]`. The 2027-batch test has not been reported by anyone, so every section figure carries Medium confidence at best. The cut-off value is not discoverable.
