## 3. Concept notes

- **One day's work = 1 / days.** A in 12 days does 1/12 per day.
- **Together = add the rates.** 1/12 + 1/15 = 9/60 = 3/20 → 20/3 days.
- **Pipes:** inlet adds, outlet (leak) subtracts.
- **Men × days = constant work** (man-days). 12 × 8 = 96 man-days; with 16 men: 96 / 16 = 6 days.
- **Shortcut for two people:** days together = ab / (a + b).

## 4. Must-memorise block

| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Together | ab / (a + b) |
| Fill and leak | ab / (b − a) where b > a |
| Work | rate × time |
| Man-days | men × days = constant |

## 5. Worked examples

**Example 1.** A 12 days, B 15 days: 12 × 15 / 27 = 180/27 = **6⅔ days**.

**Example 2.** A tap fills in 10 h, a leak empties in 15 h: 10 × 15 / 5 = **30 hours**.

**Example 3.** B works 5 days of a 20-day job, A (15 days alone) finishes: left 3/4; A needs 3/4 × 15 = **11.25 days**.

**Example 4.** 12 men, 8 days → 96 man-days; 16 men → **6 days**.

## 6. Traps and gotchas

| Trap | Why it catches people | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Adding days instead of rates | 12 + 15 | Always convert to per-day work |
| Forgetting that a leak subtracts | Treating it as a second tap | Sign: − |
| Mixing man-days and days | 12 men × 8 days is 96 man-days | Keep units |
| Partial work | Fractions of the job | Subtract done work from 1 |

**7.** **[Men and days]** 12 men can build a wall in 5 days. How many days will 4 men take to build the same wall (all work at the same rate)?
a) 23
b) 17
c) 24
d) 15

**8.** **[Two workers]** A can finish a job in 15 days and B can finish it in 12 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish it?
a) 7.67
b) 6.67
c) 13.5
d) 60

### ANSWER KEY

1. **c** — Per day A does 1/30 and B 1/45; together 1/18 of the job. Days = 1 ÷ that = **18**.
2. **d** — Net per hour = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60. Time = **60 hours**.
3. **a** — B does 4/12 = 1/3. Left: 2/3. A does 1/60 per day, so 2/3 × 60 = **40 days**.
4. **a** — Net per hour = 1/6 − 1/30 = 2/15. Time = **7.5 hours**.
5. **c** — B does 2/60 = 1/30. Left: 29/30. A does 1/15 per day, so 29/30 × 15 = **14.5 days**.
6. **b** — B does 5/12 = 5/12. Left: 7/12. A does 1/40 per day, so 7/12 × 40 = **23.33 days**.
7. **d** — Total work = 12 × 5 = 60 man-days. With 4 men: 60/4 = **15 days**.
8. **b** — Per day A does 1/15 and B 1/12; together 3/20 of the job. Days = 1 ÷ that = **6.67**.
