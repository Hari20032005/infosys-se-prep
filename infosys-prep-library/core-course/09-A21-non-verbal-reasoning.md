# Lesson 09. Non-verbal reasoning

*Topic `A21` · about 1.1 of the 75 marks · plan about 0.4 hours.*

## 1. The idea

Non-verbal reasoning means puzzles with shapes, cubes, dice, clocks and grids, where you work from a picture and not from words. In a test on a computer, the picture is sometimes replaced by a short description. That is how this lesson treats it.

The topic is a grab-bag, but only a few questions come up again and again: painted cubes cut into small cubes, the faces of a die, the mirror image of a clock, and counting squares and rectangles in a grid. Each has a short formula, and each formula can be understood in a few lines.

So this lesson is short, it costs about 25 minutes, and you should do it last in the reasoning group.

## 3. The method

1. **Decide which of the four types it is.** Painted cube, die, clock, or counting figures.
2. **Painted cube.** A big cube n by n by n is painted outside and cut into n³ small cubes. Sort the small cubes by where they sit:
    - corners (3 painted faces): always 8;
    - edges (2 painted faces): 12 × (n − 2);
    - face centres (1 painted face): 6 × (n − 2)²;
    - inside (0 painted faces): (n − 2)³.
3. **A die.** On a standard die, opposite faces add up to 7. So 1 is opposite 6, 2 opposite 5, 3 opposite 4.
4. **A mirror clock.** If the mirror stands beside the clock, left and right swap. The mirror time is 12:00 minus the time shown, on a 12-hour clock.
5. **Counting figures in an n by n grid.**
    - Squares of every size: 1² + 2² + … + n².
    - Rectangles of every kind (squares count too): the number of ways to choose 2 of the n + 1 vertical lines, times the same for horizontal lines.
6. **Check with a small case.** Does your formula work for n = 2? Count by hand. It takes ten seconds.

## 4. Shortcuts and when they fail

**Small-case check.** For a 3 × 3 × 3 cube, the pieces are 8, 12, 6 and 1, which add to 27. Use this to catch mistakes. *It fails only if you use the wrong n.*

**Total check.** The four counts for a painted cube must add up to n³. *It fails if you miss a type of piece.*

**Rectangles from squares.** Many people count squares and forget the other rectangles. In a 2 × 2 grid there are 5 squares but 9 rectangles. *It fails if the question says "squares only".*

**Mirror clock at 6:00 and 12:00.** These look the same in the mirror. *It fails for every other time.*

## 5. Worked examples

All the numbers were checked by counting piece by piece in code.

**Example 1 (a painted cube).** A 4 × 4 × 4 cube is painted and cut into 64 small cubes. How many have exactly one painted face?

Face-centre cubes: 6 faces × (4 − 2)² = 6 × 4 = **24**. Check all four counts: 8 corners, 24 on edges, 24 on faces, 8 inside, and 8 + 24 + 24 + 8 = 64. The edge count is 12 × 2 = 24 and the inside is 2³ = 8.

**Example 2 (a die).** A standard die shows 3 on top. What is on the bottom?

Opposite faces add to 7, so the bottom shows 7 − 3 = **4**.

**Example 3 (a mirror clock).** A clock shows 4:20. What time does its mirror image show?

12:00 minus 4:20 is 7:40. Answer: **7:40**.

**Example 4 (squares in a grid).** How many squares of every size are there in a 4 × 4 grid?

Squares of side 1: 16. Side 2: 9. Side 3: 4. Side 4: 1. Total: 16 + 9 + 4 + 1 = **30**.

**Example 5 (rectangles in a grid).** How many rectangles, squares included, are there in a 4 × 4 grid?

There are 5 vertical lines and 5 horizontal lines. Choose 2 of the 5 vertical lines: 10 ways. Choose 2 of the 5 horizontal lines: 10 ways. Total: 10 × 10 = **100**.

## 6. Traps

- **Counting only squares.** Read whether the question wants squares or all rectangles.
- **Forgetting the corners.** Corners have three painted faces. The edge formula excludes them.
- **Subtracting the clock the wrong way round.** Mirror time is 12:00 minus the time, not the time minus 12:00.
- **Assuming every die is standard.** The sum of 7 is true for a standard die. Use it only when the question says "standard" or when it is the usual dice problem.
- **Using the formulas without checking.** A tiny case is a quick safety net.


## 7. Practice questions

Attempt each level before opening the key. Level 3 is harder than anything in the 2020 official sample, on purpose: if you can do Level 3, the real paper will feel easy.

### Level 1: warm-up

*A die, painted-cube pieces and a simple mirror clock.*

**2.** **[Dice]** On a standard die opposite faces add up to 7. If the face showing 6 is on top, which number is on the **bottom**?

**15.** **[Counting figures]** How many squares of every size are there in an 6 × 6 grid of unit squares?  
a) 91  
b) 97  
c) 36  
d) 21  

### ANSWER KEY

1. **d** — Opposite faces sum to 7, so the bottom is 7 − 5 = **2**.
2. **a** — Opposite faces sum to 7, so the bottom is 7 − 6 = **1**.
3. **c** — Face-centre cubes: 6 faces × (6 − 2)² = **96**.
4. **b** — The mirror image time is 12:00 − 9:00 = 3:00 (subtract from 12 hours; for 12-hour clocks this is the same as 11:60 − t). Answer **3:00**.
5. **b** — Only the 8 corner cubes touch three faces: **8**.
6. **c** — Face-centre cubes: 6 faces × (3 − 2)² = **6**.
7. **b** — The mirror image time is 12:00 − 6:20 = 5:40 (subtract from 12 hours; for 12-hour clocks this is the same as 11:60 − t). Answer **5:40**.
8. **d** — Choose 2 of the 4 vertical lines and 2 of the 4 horizontal lines: C(4,2)² = 6² = **36**.
9. **a** — Edge cubes (not corners): 12 edges × (3 − 2) = **12**.
10. **b** — Choose 2 of the 5 vertical lines and 2 of the 5 horizontal lines: C(5,2)² = 10² = **100**.
11. **d** — Choose 2 of the 8 vertical lines and 2 of the 8 horizontal lines: C(8,2)² = 28² = **784**.
12. **a** — Squares of side s: (7 − s + 1)². Adding for s = 1..7: 49 + 36 + 25 + 16 + 9 + 4 + 1 = **140**.
13. **c** — The mirror image time is 12:00 − 1:50 = 10:10 (subtract from 12 hours; for 12-hour clocks this is the same as 11:60 − t). Answer **10:10**.
14. **c** — Face-centre cubes: 6 faces × (5 − 2)² = **54**.
15. **a** — Squares of side s: (6 − s + 1)². Adding for s = 1..6: 36 + 25 + 16 + 9 + 4 + 1 = **91**.

## 8. Recap in 60 seconds

- No real Infosys question of this kind has been found. Learn it last.
- Painted cube n × n × n: corners 8, edges 12(n − 2), faces 6(n − 2)², inside (n − 2)³. They add to n³.
- Standard die: opposite faces add to 7.
- Mirror clock: 12:00 minus the time shown.
- Grid squares: 1² + 2² + … + n². Grid rectangles: choose 2 vertical lines times choose 2 horizontal lines.
- Always check a tiny case.
