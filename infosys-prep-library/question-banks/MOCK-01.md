# MOCK-01 — Full-length Systems Engineer mock test

**Structure** (the observed Systems Engineer pattern, `research/03-pattern-synthesis.md` §1): 7 sections, 60 questions, **120 minutes**, **75 marks plus an essay**. No negative marking has been reported, so answer every question.

| # | Section | Questions | Marks | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reasoning Ability | 15 | 15 | 25 |
| 2 | Technical Ability (mathematical) | 10 | 10 | 35 |
| 3 | Verbal Ability | 20 | 20 | 20 |
| 4 | Pseudocode | 5 | 10 | 10 |
| 5 | Numerical Puzzle | 4 | 10 (2 + 2 + 3 + 3) | 10 |
| 6 | English Grammar | 5 | 10 | 10 |
| 7 | English Writing | 1 | unpublished | 10 |

**How to sit it.** One sitting, phone away, **no calculator**, rough paper only. Set a timer for each section and **stop when it ends**, even mid-question: the real sections are timed and cannot be revisited `[LIKELY]`. Do not look at `MOCK-01-ANSWERS.md` until the essay is finished.

**Pace.** Reasoning ≈ 100 s a question · Technical ≈ 3.5 min · Verbal ≈ 60 s · Pseudocode ≈ 2 min · Puzzle ≈ 2.5 min · Grammar ≈ 2 min.

**46.** **[Trace]** What is the output of the following pseudocode? (All variables are integers; `/` is integer division; `MOD` is the remainder.)

```text
1) Integer i, n, s

3) Set n = 13
4) For (each i from 1 to n)
5)   If (i MOD 4 == 1) then
6)     s = s + i * 2
7)   End-if
8) End-for
9) Print s
```

a) 56
b) 57
c) 58
d) 55

**47.** **[Trace]** What is the output of the following pseudocode? (All variables are integers; `/` is integer division; `MOD` is the remainder.)

```text
1) Integer c, i, j
2) Set c = 0
3) Set j = 0
4) For (each i from 1 to 3)
5)   For (each j from i to 7)
6)     If ((i + j) MOD 2 == 0) then
7)       c++
8)     End-if
9)   End-for
10) End-for
11) Print c
```

a) 10
b) 8
c) 11
d) 9

**48.** **[Increment operators]** What is the value of `result` after this code runs? Java-style left-to-right evaluation.


b) 0
c) 1
d) 2

**49.** **[Recursion]** What is the output of the following pseudocode?

```text

    if (y == 0)

```

b) 48
c) 11
d) 24

**50.** **[Bitwise]** What is the output of the following pseudocode? (`&` is bitwise AND, `|` OR, `^` XOR.)
