### ANSWER KEY

1. **d** — *Artificial intelligence* is singular, and the sentence describes something happening now: **is** changing.
2. **b** — *Tasks* is plural and *now* fixes the present: **are** done.
3. **d** — *Leaves employees more time*: the comparative **more**.
4. **b** — The two clauses contrast (fear vs. new roles): **Although**.
5. **c** — *Workers … are best prepared*: plural subject, present tense.
6. **d** — The fixed phrase is *one of the most …*: **one**.
7. **c** — The noun *access* takes **to**.
8. **a** — *Pipes* is plural and the passage is in the present: **are** old.
9. **a** — A singular subject and a plan announced for the future: present perfect **has** announced.
10. **c** — In an *if*-clause the present simple is used, and *everyone* is singular: **uses**.
11. **d** — You travel **to** a place.
12. **a** — *Was scheduled* is followed by an infinitive: **to leave**.
13. **d** — The train left *before* I arrived: two past events, the earlier one in the past perfect, **had** already left.
14. **a** — A length of time after *wait*: **for** three hours.
15. **b** — *Taught me to always plan* (infinitive of purpose): **to**.
16. **a** — The fixed pair is *good for*.
17. **d** — The fixed phrase is *at least*.
18. **b** — *People who exercise*: a relative pronoun for people, subject of *exercise*: **who**.
19. **c** — After the preposition *of* use the object form: *many of us*.
20. **b** — A modal is followed by the base verb: *can make*.
21. **c** — *In the last few years*. (*Since* needs a point in time.)
22. **a** — A possessive before a noun: **their** homes.
23. **b** — *It* is singular and the statement is general: **requires**.
24. **c** — Singular subject *student*, present tense: **gets**.
25. **a** — The teachers' response follows from the problem just described: **Therefore**.
