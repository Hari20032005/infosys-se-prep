# QB-V2 — Identify the correct sentence

**Verbal Ability** · **Tier 1** · 8 questions · topic file: see the `V2` file in `topics/`, `puzzles/`, `pseudocode/` or `interview/`

The official sample paper has an error-identification set in this format: four versions of a sentence, one correct. Hand-written for this library.

**How to use this bank.** Attempt every question before opening the key. Answer letters are balanced across a–d and not in a repeating pattern, so guessing the pattern will not help.

## Practice questions

**1.** **[Correct sentence]** Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
a) Each of the players was given a medal.
b) Each of the players have given a medal.
c) Each of the players were given a medal.
d) Each of the players were given medals.

**2.** **[Correct sentence]** Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
a) She worked here since five years.
b) She has been working here for five years.
c) She has been working here since five years.
d) She is working here since five years.

**3.** **[Correct sentence]** Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
a) The numbers of student is increasing every year.
b) The number of students is increasing each year.
c) The number of students are increasing every year.
d) The number of student are increasing every year.

**4.** **[Correct sentence]** Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
a) He does not knew the answer.
b) He did not knew the answer.
c) He did not known the answer.
d) He did not know the answer.

**5.** **[Correct sentence]** Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
a) I am looking forward to meet you next week.
b) I look forward to meet you next week.
c) I am looking forward for meeting you next week.
d) I am looking forward to meeting you next week.

**6.** **[Correct sentence]** Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
a) If I knew, I would have helped.
b) If I would have known, I would have helped.
c) If I had known, I would have helped.
d) If I have known, I would helped.

**7.** **[Correct sentence]** Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
a) The information you gave me was very useful.
b) The informations you gave me were very useful.
c) The information you gave me were very useful.
d) The informations you gave me was very useful.

**8.** **[Correct sentence]** Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
a) He is seniorer than me.
b) He is senior than me.
c) He is senior to me.
d) He is more senior than me.

### ANSWER KEY

1. **a** — *Each* is singular: ‘was given’.
2. **b** — A length of time takes *for*; *since* needs a point in time.
3. **b** — *The number of* takes a singular verb, and the noun after *of* is plural: ‘students’.
4. **d** — After *did not* the verb is in its base form: ‘know’.
5. **d** — *Look forward to* is followed by the -ing form.
6. **c** — Third conditional: *if* + past perfect, *would have* + past participle.
7. **a** — *Information* is uncountable (no plural) and takes a singular verb.
8. **c** — *Senior* is followed by *to*, not *than*.
