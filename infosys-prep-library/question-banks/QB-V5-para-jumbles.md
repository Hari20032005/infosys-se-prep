# QB-V5 — Para-jumbles

**Verbal Ability** · **Tier 3** · 8 questions · topic file: see the `V5` file in `topics/`, `puzzles/`, `pseudocode/` or `interview/`

Hand-written for this library. Each set is a chain in which every sentence points back to the one before it, so exactly one order works.

**How to use this bank.** Attempt every question before opening the key. Answer letters are balanced across a–d and not in a repeating pattern, so guessing the pattern will not help.

---

## Practice questions

**1.** **[Para-jumble]** Rearrange the four sentences into a meaningful paragraph. Choose the correct order.

P. Cricket is played in more than a hundred countries.
Q. Since then it has brought millions of new fans to the game.
R. This format was introduced in the early 1970s.
S. Its most popular form is the one-day match.

a) PSRQ
b) SRQP
c) PSQR
d) SPRQ

**2.** **[Para-jumble]** Rearrange the four sentences into a meaningful paragraph. Choose the correct order.

P. Finally, close politely and name the action you need.
Q. It tells the reader what to expect before opening the message.
R. The body should then state the request in the first two sentences.
S. A good email begins with a clear subject line.

a) QRPS
b) QSRP
c) SQPR
d) SQRP

**3.** **[Para-jumble]** Rearrange the four sentences into a meaningful paragraph. Choose the correct order.

P. This rock, called magma, rises through cracks in the crust.
Q. Volcanoes form where hot rock from deep inside the Earth reaches the surface.
R. Over time, these materials build the familiar cone-shaped mountain.
S. When the pressure becomes too high, it erupts as lava, ash and gas.

a) PQSR
b) PSRQ
c) QPSR
d) QPRS

**4.** **[Para-jumble]** Rearrange the four sentences into a meaningful paragraph. Choose the correct order.

P. That is why many schools now include swimming in their programmes.
Q. Learning to swim is an important life skill.
R. It builds strength, and it may one day save a life.
S. Children who start early in such programmes usually become confident swimmers.

a) RPSQ
b) QRPS
c) QRSP
d) RQPS

**5.** **[Para-jumble]** Rearrange the four sentences into a meaningful paragraph. Choose the correct order.

P. Therefore the railways spend large sums on safety and new lines.
Q. Such numbers place heavy demands on the network.
R. Every day it carries millions of passengers across the country.
S. Train travel remains the backbone of transport in India.

a) SRPQ
b) SRQP
c) RQPS
d) RSQP

**6.** **[Para-jumble]** Rearrange the four sentences into a meaningful paragraph. Choose the correct order.

P. Teamwork is one of the most valued skills in a workplace.
Q. No single employee can complete a large project alone.
R. Such people usually advance faster in their careers.
S. Hence, employers look for people who can share tasks and credit.

a) QPSR
b) PQRS
c) PQSR
d) QSRP

**7.** **[Para-jumble]** Rearrange the four sentences into a meaningful paragraph. Choose the correct order.

P. This electricity can run homes without burning any fuel.
Q. As a result, the use of rooftop panels has grown fast in recent years.
R. Solar energy is among the cleanest sources of power.
S. Panels on rooftops convert sunlight directly into electricity.

a) SRPQ
b) SPQR
c) RSQP
d) RSPQ

**8.** **[Para-jumble]** Rearrange the four sentences into a meaningful paragraph. Choose the correct order.

P. Customers can now compare prices in seconds from anywhere.
Q. This pressure has forced shops to offer better deals.
R. In the end, buyers are the ones who gain.
S. Mobile phones have changed how people shop.

a) SPQR
b) PSQR
c) SPRQ
d) PQRS

### ANSWER KEY

1. **a** — Correct order: **PSRQ**. *Its* = cricket. *This format* = one-day. *Since then* needs the 1970s date.
2. **d** — Correct order: **SQRP**. *It* = the subject line. *then* follows the subject line; *Finally* ends.
3. **c** — Correct order: **QPSR**. *This rock, called magma* needs the hot rock; *it erupts* follows the pressure; *these materials* needs lava, ash and gas.
4. **b** — Correct order: **QRPS**. *It* = swimming. *That is why* needs the benefits. *Such programmes* needs *programmes*.
5. **b** — Correct order: **SRQP**. *It* = train travel. *Such numbers* = millions of passengers. *Therefore* draws the conclusion.
6. **c** — Correct order: **PQSR**. *Hence* follows the statement that no one can work alone. *Such people* = those who share tasks.
7. **d** — Correct order: **RSPQ**. *This electricity* needs the panels sentence. *As a result* concludes.
8. **a** — Correct order: **SPQR**. *This pressure* = price comparison. *In the end* concludes.
