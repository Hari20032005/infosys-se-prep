# QB-W1 — Writing prompts

**English Writing** · **Tier 1** · 15 prompts

The test gives one essay or email in 10 minutes. Use each prompt as a timed drill, then compare your structure with the outline.

---

## Prompts

**1.** **[Essay]** Ozone layer depletion
*Outline:* Define the layer → CFC cause → effects on health and crops → Montreal Protocol and recovery → a personal step.

**2.** **[Essay]** The future of remote work
*Outline:* Define remote work → benefits (commute, balance, talent) → challenges (isolation, communication) → hybrid model → conclusion.

**3.** **[Essay]** Plastic pollution
*Outline:* Scale of the problem → causes (single-use, weak collection) → effects (drains, rivers, animals) → solutions (reuse, recycling, rules).

**4.** **[Essay]** Artificial intelligence in everyday life
*Outline:* Where we meet it (phones, maps, recommendations) → benefits → risks (jobs, privacy, errors) → responsible use.

**5.** **[Essay]** Importance of a healthy lifestyle
*Outline:* Definition → diet, exercise, sleep → effects on study and work → habits to start today.

**6.** **[Essay]** Social media: boon or bane
*Outline:* What it offers → risks (time, comparison, misinformation) → balanced use → conclusion.

**7.** **[Essay]** Renewable energy for India
*Outline:* Need → solar, wind, hydro → challenges (storage, cost) → government role → outlook.

**8.** **[Email]** Email to your manager: you need a day off next Friday
*Outline:* Subject → purpose in line 1 → date and reason → how work will be covered → polite close.

**9.** **[Email]** Email to a vendor: the delivered laptops are faulty
*Outline:* Subject with order number → what arrived → what is wrong → what you want (replacement by a date) → close.

**10.** **[Email]** Email to your professor: request an extension for a project
*Outline:* Subject → purpose → reason in one sentence → revised date → thanks.

**11.** **[Email]** Email to HR confirming your joining date
*Outline:* Subject → thank for the offer → confirm the date and location → list documents you will bring → close.

**12.** **[Essay]** Cyber safety for students
*Outline:* Risks → simple rules (passwords, links, updates) → role of the college → conclusion.

**13.** **[Essay]** Teamwork in the workplace
*Outline:* Definition → benefits → obstacles → how to build a good team → conclusion.

**14.** **[Email]** Email to a colleague: reschedule a meeting
*Outline:* Subject → purpose → two alternative slots → apology → close.

**15.** **[Essay]** Cashless payments and digital India
*Outline:* Growth of UPI → benefits → risks (fraud, digital divide) → what to do → outlook.

### Self-marking checklist

- On topic, with a clear introduction and conclusion
- Three points, each in its own sentence or two
- Present tense unless describing history
- No contractions in an email
- Re-read once: agreement, articles, spelling
- About 190 words in 10 minutes.
