# 01 — Application Checklist

**Do this before you study anything.** The evaluation invitation is generated from the Infosys Application Form. No form, no invite, and a perfect preparation scores zero.

College nomination closed **Thu 24 Sep 2026**. The form can arrive any day from now.

---

## 1. Watch the right inbox

| # | Action | Done |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The form goes to your **personal** email address. College-domain (`@vitstudent.ac.in`) addresses are rejected by Infosys | ☐ |
| 2 | Check **Inbox, Spam, Promotions and Updates** every morning and evening until it arrives | ☐ |
| 3 | Search the mailbox for `Infosys`, `InfyTQ`, `Application Form`, `campus`, `SuccessFactors` and `noreply` | ☐ |
| 4 | Mark the sender as "not spam" / add to contacts so the evaluation invite lands in Inbox | ☐ |
| 5 | Confirm with the placement cell that the personal address they submitted in the nomination is the one you are watching. A typo there is the most likely reason a form never arrives | ☐ |

`[INFERRED]` — the sender domain and the platform the form sits on are not stated in the official email. The search terms above cover the names Infosys has used for candidate portals; the placement cell's copy of the email is the authority.

## 2. Complete it the day it lands

The invitation is "sent based on the details in that form". Treat the form as the first round.

- Fill it **the same day**. There is no stated deadline, which means you should assume a short one.
- Use the **same personal email** everywhere in the form.
- Enter marks exactly as they appear on the marksheets. Mismatches surface at document verification, long after the test.
- Screenshot or save the confirmation page and any confirmation email.

## 3. What to keep ready before it arrives

| Item | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 10th and 12th marksheets | PDF scan | Percentage or CGPA as printed |
| Semester grade sheets to date | PDF scan | Integrated M.Tech: all semesters so far |
| Current CGPA | Number | As on the latest official grade sheet |
| Government photo ID | PDF scan | Aadhaar or PAN. Name must match the marksheets |
| Passport-size photo | JPG, plain background | Many forms cap size at 100–200 KB |
| Resume | PDF, one page | Projects with Java/SQL named explicitly |
| Registration number, programme name, expected graduation month | Text | "Integrated M.Tech Software Engineering, May 2027" |
| Any gap-year or backlog details | Text | Answer truthfully; these are checked at onboarding |

`[UNVERIFIED — model inference]` — the list above is what campus application forms of this kind usually ask for. The real form is the authority.

## 4. If the form has not arrived

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| 2 days after nomination closed (26 Sep) | Re-check spam and search terms. Confirm with classmates whether theirs has arrived |
| If classmates have theirs and you do not | Email the **VIT CDC / placement cell** with your registration number and the personal email you submitted. Ask them to confirm you are on the nominated list and the address is correct |
| If nobody has received it | Nothing to do yet. Keep studying; the form is batch-sent |

Do not write to Infosys directly unless the placement cell tells you to. The nomination list is the college's, and they are the ones who can fix it.

## 5. After the form — the evaluation invite

- The invite will state the date, venue and reporting time. The evaluation is **in person, on campus**.
- Read it for: what to carry (ID, admit card, printouts), device rules, and whether a laptop is needed.
- Record the date in `99-STUDY-PLAN.md` and pick the 3-, 7- or 14-day variant from the days remaining.
